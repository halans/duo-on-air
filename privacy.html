Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

In short: DuoOnAir does not collect, transmit, share, or sell any personal data. Your settings stay on your device.

Information the app collects

None. DuoOnAir has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. It does not include any third-party software development kits (SDKs), and it does not connect to the internet.

Information stored on your device

To remember your preferences, DuoOnAir saves its settings on your device using the standard iOS settings storage. This includes:

  • the message you selected, and any custom text you typed;
  • your chosen color, text style, font, and animation;
  • display options such as the background grid, corner glow, line limit, and appearance.

This information never leaves your device through DuoOnAir. It may be included in device backups that you make with iCloud or a computer, which are governed by your own backup settings and Apple's privacy policy. Deleting the app removes these settings.

Permissions

DuoOnAir does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information. While the app is open, it keeps the screen from going to sleep so the sign stays lit; this uses no personal data.

Children's privacy

Because DuoOnAir collects no personal data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics, or tracking scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement for details.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, email support@halans.dev.
