Turn the outer screen of your iPhone Duo into a glowing On Air sign.

Stand it up in tent mode and let everyone know you're recording, in a meeting, or just busy.

Coming soon to the App Store

Features

  • Fills the screen

    Messages scale to fill the outer display and wrap onto up to three lines when that makes them bigger.

  • Neon animations

    Glow, Pulse, Breathe, Blink, or Neon Flicker, with a single faulty letter that stutters like a real tube.

  • Your colors and fonts

    Red, amber, green, blue, pink, white or any custom color. Solid, outline, or rainbow letters in seven typefaces.

  • Stays on

    The screen doesn't sleep while the sign is showing, so it stays lit for your whole call or recording.

  • Swipe to switch

    Swipe left or right on the sign to change the message without opening settings.

  • Accessible

    Respects Reduce Motion, keeps flashing below safe limits, and supports VoiceOver.

Ready-made messages

  • ON AIR
  • DND
  • RECORDING
  • BUSY
  • MEETING
  • NOT NOW
  • YOUR OWN TEXT

How it works

  1. Open DuoOnAir and tap the sign to choose a message, color, font and animation.
  2. Fold your iPhone Duo into tent mode so the outer screen faces the room.
  3. The sign stays lit until you close the app.
