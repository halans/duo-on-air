ON
AIR

Turn the outer screen of your iPhone Duo into a glowing On Air sign.

Stand it up in tent mode and let everyone know you're recording, in a meeting, or just busy.

Coming soon to the App Store

Features

  • Fills the screen

    Messages scale to fill the outer display, wrapping onto two or three lines when that makes them bigger, and stay centered clear of the camera.

  • Neon animations

    Glow, Pulse, Breathe, Blink, or Neon Flicker, with a single faulty letter that stutters like a real tube. Speed is adjustable.

  • Your colors

    Red, amber, green, blue, pink, white or any custom color. Solid, outline, or rainbow letters.

  • Seven typefaces

    Classic, Rounded and Mono (SF Pro), Helvetica Neue, JetBrains Mono and Space Grotesk.

  • Backdrop

    Add a faint background grid and a corner glow in the text color.

  • Stays on

    The screen doesn't sleep while the sign is showing, so it stays lit for your whole call or recording.

  • Swipe to switch

    Swipe left or right on the sign to move through the messages in a category without opening settings.

  • Accessible

    Reduce Motion replaces flashing and pulsing with a steady sign, flicker stays under three flashes a second, and VoiceOver can read and change the message.

  • Dark or light

    Choose light, dark or system appearance for settings. The sign itself is always black.

How it works

  1. Open DuoOnAir and tap the sign to choose a message, color, font and animation.
  2. Fold your iPhone Duo into tent mode so the outer screen faces the room.
  3. The sign stays lit until you close the app.
